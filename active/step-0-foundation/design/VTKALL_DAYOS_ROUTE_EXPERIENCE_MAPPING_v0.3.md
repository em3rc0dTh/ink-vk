# 12. Learning from v0.3

A donor may reveal that the target already has enough latent information to justify a route missing from the current implementation.

But route introduction requires:

```text
TARGET INFORMATION JOB
+
TARGET CONTENT DEPTH
+
KNOWN CLAIM CEILING
+
SOUL FIT
```

not merely donor topology.

Company passes that test.

Partnership still does not.

This is an important distinction:

> **Ink-VK may discover a missing target route; it may not invent a missing target institution.**
