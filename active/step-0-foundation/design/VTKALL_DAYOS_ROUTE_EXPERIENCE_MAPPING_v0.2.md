## 1. Why v0.2 exists

v0.1 made one important overreach.

It treated Dayos route topology as a strong enough reason to propose target routes such as:

```text
PARTNERSHIP
CAPABILITIES detail family
multiple standalone SOLUTION detail routes
```

The user correction is authoritative:

> High donor fidelity does not authorize invented target business structure.

Therefore v0.2 applies the Ink-VK `Target Truth Gate`.

The new rule is:

> **Match Dayos structure where VertikALL has a real information job and sufficient target truth. Omit or defer donor routes whose target meaning is not established.**
