
## 4. Evidence boundary

These captures establish what was visibly rendered at the captured viewport sizes and what the user explicitly marked during the visual review.

They do **not** by themselves prove:

- the behavior at other viewport sizes;
- the exact DOM/CSS cause of each visual issue;
- animation or transition behavior not visible in a still capture;
- whether a clipped item becomes fully visible after additional scrolling;
- whether an issue is caused by implementation, browser zoom, viewport dimensions, or an intentional composition decision.

Those questions belong to later correlation with the recovered code and, when appropriate, runtime inspection.
