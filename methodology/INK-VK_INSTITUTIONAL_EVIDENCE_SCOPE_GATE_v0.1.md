# Ink-VK — Institutional Evidence Scope Gate v0.1

**Status:** PROPOSED / CROSS-CASE CANDIDATE  
**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Learning source:** VTKALL Company × Dayos Company  
**Scope:** About / Company / Team / Partners / Careers / Trust / News / institutional-proof surfaces

---

## 1. Problem

Target Truth prevents unsupported facts from entering a transformed product.

Company-style routes expose a subtler failure:

```text
A TARGET HAS A LOT OF TRUE INFORMATION
BUT
THAT INFORMATION BELONGS TO ONE EVIDENCE DOMAIN
NOT ANOTHER
```

Example:

```text
MISSION / ORIGIN / WORLDVIEW = STRONGLY SUPPORTED
```

but:

```text
TEAM SIZE / PARTNER LOGOS / GENDER STATS / OPEN ROLES / LATEST NEWS
= NOT ESTABLISHED
```

Without an evidence-scope check, a rich donor Company page can cause this invalid inference:

```text
DEEP TARGET STORY
        ↓
TARGET FEELS LIKE A REAL COMPANY
        ↓
FILL ALL COMPANY-PAGE SLOTS
        ↓
SYNTHETIC INSTITUTIONAL PROOF
```

Governing rule:

> **Narrative depth does not imply institutional proof depth.**

---

# 2. Relationship to Target Truth

Target Truth asks:

```text
IS THIS TARGET CLAIM / CONTENT SUPPORTED?
```

Institutional Evidence Scope asks:

```text
SUPPORTED IN WHICH DOMAIN?
FOR WHICH ENTITY?
AT WHAT CURRENTNESS?
FOR WHICH PUBLIC USE?
```

The gate is therefore a refinement of Truth, not a replacement.

Do not create a separate macro phase.

---

# 3. Institutional evidence domains

Use the following working domains when a donor contains company/institutional proof jobs.

## I0 — IDENTITY / NARRATIVE

Examples:

```text
mission
worldview
origin meaning
values
philosophy
purpose
relationship model
what the entity is / is not
future thesis
```

Evidence may be deep without proving institutional scale.

---

## I1 — ORGANIZATIONAL FACT

Examples:

```text
legal / entity identity
founded date
company location
office location
organization structure
operating geography
historical milestones
```

These require direct entity-specific evidence.

Historical truth may also need publication approval.

---

## I2 — PEOPLE PROOF

Examples:

```text
team roster
leaders
bios
roles
headcount
portraits
team structure
```

A mission statement never proves I2.

---

## I3 — RELATIONSHIP / EXTERNAL PROOF

Examples:

```text
customer logos
partner logos
investors
formal alliances
awards
certifications
press proof
external endorsements
```

Relationship philosophy is I0.

Partner proof is I3.

They are not interchangeable.

---

## I4 — QUANTITATIVE INSTITUTIONAL PROOF

Examples:

```text
employee count
growth
location distribution
gender / demographic statistics
customer counts
partner counts
revenue / traction
market metrics
```

Conceptual diagrams do not qualify.

---

## I5 — ACTIVE INSTITUTIONAL STATE

Examples:

```text
open jobs
active hiring
current programs
current partnerships
latest updates
current events
recent news
current leadership
active office footprint
```

I5 has a strong freshness requirement.

A historically true document may not be sufficient.

---

# 4. Evidence-scope matrix

For each institutional donor job record:

```text
DONOR JOB
TARGET EQUIVALENT JOB
TARGET ENTITY
EVIDENCE DOMAIN
SOURCE
EVIDENCE CLASS (T0–T4)
FRESHNESS NEED
CURRENTNESS VERIFIED?
PUBLIC-SAFETY
CLAIM CEILING
DISPOSITION
```

Possible dispositions remain:

```text
TRANSFER
REFRAME
OMIT
DEFER
```

---

# 5. Entity authority rule

Institutional evidence must belong to the target entity being represented.

Examples:

```text
PARENT / ECOSYSTEM EXPERIENCE
≠ TARGET TEAM SIZE

RELATED COMPANY CUSTOMER
≠ TARGET CUSTOMER

FOUNDER HISTORY
≠ TARGET COMPANY TRACTION

ECOSYSTEM PARTNER
≠ TARGET FORMAL PARTNER
```

If related-entity evidence is relevant, record:

```text
SOURCE ENTITY
RELATIONSHIP TO TARGET
PUBLIC AUTHORITY
ALLOWED CLAIM
```

Do not collapse ecosystem boundaries.

---

# 6. Narrative-to-proof anti-smuggling rule

A valid I0 claim cannot authorize I1–I5 proof.

```text
"We value long-term relationships"
≠ partner logo wall

"We came from years of operational experience"
≠ target company age metric

"Everything is a network"
≠ target location map

"We care about people"
≠ team portraits

"We are still growing"
≠ active careers program

"Our future is beginning"
≠ Latest Updates feed
```

This is a mandatory review check for Company/About routes.

---

# 7. Proof-role preservation without fake proof

A donor may use a visual device for institutional proof:

```text
logos
data graphics
photography
timeline
team grid
news cards
careers CTA
```

When the target lacks corresponding evidence:

```text
PRESERVE THE UNDERLYING JOB ONLY IF A TRUTHFUL TARGET EQUIVALENT EXISTS
```

Do not preserve empty proof chrome for its own sake.

Examples:

### Partner logo wall

Target has only relationship philosophy:

```text
PROOF JOB → NOT SATISFIED
NARRATIVE JOB → MAY BE REFRAMED ELSEWHERE
LOGO WALL → OMIT
```

### Demographic chart

Target has conceptual network material but no demographic statistics:

```text
STATISTICAL PROOF JOB → OMIT
CONCEPTUAL DIAGRAM → MAY EXIST AS A DIFFERENT JOB
```

The conceptual diagram must never visually impersonate data proof.

---

# 8. Photography truth rule

Real photography can carry several different jobs:

```text
PEOPLE PROOF
PLACE PROOF
CUSTOMER PROOF
CULTURE PROOF
EDITORIAL ATMOSPHERE
```

For proof jobs, require target-owned and public-safe imagery.

Forbidden substitutions:

```text
stock team photo → target team proof
unrelated workspace → target office proof
supplier image → target customer proof
AI-generated people → real team proof
```

Generated/conceptual imagery may be used only when clearly serving a conceptual/editorial role, not documentary proof.

---

# 9. Freshness rule

Some institutional facts are relatively stable:

```text
mission
origin thesis
historical event
```

Others decay quickly:

```text
current team
headcount
open roles
partners
leadership
latest updates
offices
program status
```

For freshness-sensitive jobs record:

```text
AS-OF DATE
SOURCE DATE
VERIFICATION DATE
OWNER / AUTHORITY
```

If currentness is not verified:

```text
DEFER
```

Do not convert a stale truth into a current claim.

---

# 10. "Latest" rule

Any label such as:

```text
Latest Updates
Current Team
Open Roles
Our Partners
Today
Now
```

contains a current-state claim.

It requires:

```text
current source authority
real records
real timestamps/status
refresh discipline
```

A donor's dynamic/current section cannot be populated from arbitrary old content just to complete the layout.

---

# 11. Company-route capacity can be asymmetric

A target may have:

```text
I0 narrative capacity        → HIGH
I1 organizational facts      → MEDIUM
I2 people proof               → C0
I3 relationship proof         → C0
I4 quantitative proof         → C0
I5 active institutional state → C0
```

This is valid.

The resulting page may be editorially rich and institutionally sparse.

Rule:

> **Institutional completeness is not required for narrative completeness.**

Do not invent missing domains to make a page feel more mature.

---

# 12. New-route non-regression scope

Company pages may be introduced where no prior target route existed.

In that case:

```text
ROUTE-SPECIFIC NON-REGRESSION BASELINE → NONE
```

Do not fabricate route-specific legacy defects.

Instead inherit:

```text
GLOBAL TARGET DEFECTS
GLOBAL POSITIVE CONTROLS
CROSS-ROUTE DESIGN INVARIANTS
```

This keeps Non-Regression evidence-based.

---

# 13. Institutional claim ceiling

For each domain define a ceiling independently.

Example:

```text
I0 IDENTITY
mission / worldview → PUBLIC-SAFE

I1 HISTORY
specific lineage/date → CONDITIONAL PUBLICATION

I2 PEOPLE
team roster → UNSUPPORTED

I3 PARTNERS
partner logos → UNSUPPORTED

I4 STATS
headcount/demographics → UNSUPPORTED

I5 CURRENT STATE
latest updates / hiring → UNSUPPORTED
```

Do not use one global `Company = verified` flag.

---

# 14. Interaction necessity under institutional scope

Institutional interactions require real target state.

Examples:

## Team filter

Requires multiple real team records/categories.

## Careers CTA

Requires a real careers destination or active contact process.

## Latest-update carousel

Requires multiple current update records.

## Partner carousel

Requires approved partner proof.

## Local editorial rail

May require only multiple real narrative sections and can therefore be valid even when institutional proof is sparse.

This is why Company navigation and Company proof must be judged separately.

---

# 15. Relationship to Donor Job Decomposition

Donor Job Decomposition asks:

```text
WHAT JOB DOES THIS DONOR SECTION PERFORM?
```

Institutional Evidence Scope asks:

```text
WHAT EVIDENCE DOMAIN WOULD THE TARGET NEED TO PERFORM THAT JOB TRUTHFULLY?
```

Example:

```text
DONOR SECTION = TEAM + PHOTOGRAPHY

DECOMPOSITION:
J4 PROOF + J6 TRUST

EVIDENCE SCOPE:
I2 PEOPLE PROOF

TARGET I2 CAPACITY = C0

RESULT:
OMIT / DEFER
```

---

# 16. Relationship to Content Capacity

Truth:

```text
Is there evidence?
```

Scope:

```text
Is it evidence of the right institutional domain/entity/currentness?
```

Capacity:

```text
Is there enough real content/state to justify the mechanism?
```

Example:

```text
one verified partner
→ I3 truth may exist
→ but capacity may still not justify a filterable partner library
```

These checks are complementary.

# 18. Gate checklist

Before admitting a Company/About/Trust institutional slot:

```text
[ ] donor job identified
[ ] target entity identified
[ ] evidence domain identified
[ ] target evidence exists
[ ] evidence belongs to target entity
[ ] evidence class recorded
[ ] public-safety known
[ ] freshness requirement known
[ ] currentness verified if needed
[ ] content capacity sufficient for mechanism
[ ] no adjacent domain is being inferred
[ ] non-regression obligations applied
```
