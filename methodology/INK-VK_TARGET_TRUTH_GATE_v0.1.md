# Ink-VK — Target Truth Gate v0.1

**Status:** PROPOSED / FOUNDATION CANDIDATE  
**Date:** 2026-08-14  
**Origin case:** VTKALL × Dayos  
**Framework role:** prevent donor structure from inventing target business reality

---

## 1. Why this gate exists

High-fidelity donor transposition creates a specific failure mode:

```text
DONOR HAS A SLOT
      ↓
TARGET DOES NOT HAVE THE FACT
      ↓
TEAM INVENTS AN ANALOGUE TO PRESERVE VISUAL PARITY
```

That is not Ink-VK.

A donor may establish that a section, interaction, visual device, page mode, proof slot, comparison, pricing surface, partner block, metric row, or route exists in the donor system.

It does **not** establish that the target has the business truth required to populate it.

The governing rule is:

> **Donor structure may create a question. Only target evidence may create target truth.**

---

## 2. Canonical rule

For every donor-derived route, section, component, proof slot, CTA, metric, comparison, customer/partner block, product surface, or 3D semantic role, Ink-VK must ask:

```text
WHAT TARGET FACT JUSTIFIES THIS?
```

If no target fact exists, the slot cannot be filled by analogy.

The valid dispositions are:

```text
FILL       — target evidence directly supports the slot
REFRAME    — donor information job is useful but target meaning differs
OMIT       — donor slot has no valid target information job
DEFER      — target job may exist, but evidence is not sufficient yet
```

`INVENT` is not a valid disposition.

---

## 3. Target evidence classes

### T0 — DIRECT TARGET FACT

Directly established in authoritative target material.

Examples:

```text
mission
approved audience
current public copy
known route/content
verified operational capability
verified business relationship
approved brand asset
```

May populate a public section if it is also public-safe.

---

### T1 — EXPLICITLY APPROVED TARGET DECISION

A target-side decision explicitly approved by the project authority but not necessarily present in the old public site.

Examples:

```text
approved new route
approved positioning
approved product name
approved public relationship model
approved CTA destination
```

May shape the target system according to the scope of approval.

Do not silently promote a brainstorm into T1.

---

### T2 — DEMONSTRATION / POC / NON-PRODUCTION EVIDENCE

Real target capability exists, but its status is limited.

Examples:

```text
demonstration environment
proof of concept
recovered technical asset
example industry pack
non-adopted system
```

It may be shown only with status-preserving language.

Required rule:

```text
DEMO ≠ CUSTOMER SUCCESS
POC ≠ PRODUCTION ADOPTION
CAPABILITY ≠ MARKET-PROVEN PRODUCT
```

---

### T3 — SOURCE-DERIVED SYNTHESIS

A new organization or label composed only from already-supported target facts, without introducing a new factual claim.

Examples:

```text
ORGANIZE / FOLLOW UP / CONTROL
```

may be a valid information taxonomy when each category is traceable to existing target language and capabilities.

T3 may structure information.

T3 must not manufacture:

```text
customers
partnerships
products
pricing
metrics
traction
market status
legal relationships
```

---

### T4 — UNKNOWN / UNSUPPORTED

No sufficient target evidence exists.

Examples:

```text
pricing not approved
partner program not established
customer metric not verified
production outcome not evidenced
commercial process unresolved
```

T4 cannot populate public truth.

Allowed actions:

```text
OMIT
DEFER
REQUEST EVIDENCE LATER
```

Unknown is a valid architectural result.

---

## 4. Donor-induced existence is forbidden

The donor having a page does not justify the target having the same page.

Examples:

```text
DAYOS HAS PLANS
≠
VERTIKALL HAS PLANS / PRICING

DAYOS HAS PARTNERSHIP
≠
VERTIKALL HAS A PARTNERSHIP PROGRAM

DAYOS HAS METRICS
≠
VERTIKALL HAS METRICS

DAYOS HAS CUSTOMER / INTEGRATION LOGOS
≠
VERTIKALL HAS PUBLIC CUSTOMER / PARTNER PROOF
```

The correct R3 behavior is:

```text
MATCH DONOR EXPERIENCE
WHERE TARGET TRUTH EXISTS
```

not:

```text
MATCH DONOR CONTENT INVENTORY
AT ANY COST
```

---

## 5. Truth-preserving structural parity

For high-fidelity modes, use:

> **Structural parity under target truth.**

This permits four transformations.

### A — Direct parity

```text
DONOR JOB = TARGET JOB
TARGET EVIDENCE EXISTS
→ MATCH
```

### B — Semantic reframing

```text
DONOR JOB IS USEFUL
TARGET HAS A DIFFERENT VALID MEANING
→ KEEP STRUCTURAL / INTERACTION ROLE
→ RE-AUTHOR SEMANTICS
```

### C — Omission

```text
DONOR SLOT HAS NO TARGET TRUTH
→ OMIT
```

### D — Deferred parity

```text
TARGET JOB APPEARS PLAUSIBLE
BUT EVIDENCE IS INSUFFICIENT
→ DEFER
```

A deferred slot is not an invitation to fill it with placeholder business claims.

## 6. Core-project ingestion rule

A broader Core Project may contain knowledge useful to a target website.

Ink-VK may use it only when the relationship is explicit.

For every Core-derived item record:

```text
SOURCE ENTITY
TARGET RELEVANCE
STATUS
PUBLIC-SAFETY
CLAIM CEILING
```

Do not flatten ecosystem knowledge into target truth.

Examples:

```text
TECH capability
≠ automatically VertikALL product

Thradex history
≠ automatically VertikALL customer proof

Gallo / Turagua demo work
≠ automatically VertikALL production success story
```

Core can raise understanding without raising the public claim ceiling.

---

## 7. Public claim ceiling

For every section define the strongest claim evidence allows.

Possible ceilings:

```text
FACT
APPROVED INTENT
CAPABILITY
DEMONSTRATION
PATTERN
HYPOTHESIS
UNKNOWN
```

The public copy must stay at or below the ceiling.

Example:

```text
Evidence: Turagua Racing / MecánicaPro demonstration environment
Allowed: demonstration / example industry pack
Not allowed: proven customer success / production result / ROI claim
```

---

## 8. Proof slots require proof

Donor proof grammar may be preserved:

```text
CLAIM
↓
MECHANISM
↓
PROOF
```

But an empty proof slot must never be filled with donor evidence or invented target evidence.

Valid responses:

```text
use verified target proof
use demonstration proof with correct label
replace proof type
omit proof slot
mark proof unresolved for blueprint
```

---

## 9. Route admission test

Before introducing a donor-inspired route, require all of:

```text
1. TARGET INFORMATION JOB EXISTS
2. SUFFICIENT TARGET CONTENT EXISTS
3. ROUTE IS NOT CREATED ONLY TO MIRROR DONOR NAVIGATION
4. PUBLIC CLAIM CEILING IS KNOWN
5. ROUTE CAN BE EXPLAINED FROM TARGET SOUL WITHOUT NAMING DONOR
```

If any of 1–3 fail, the route is not admitted.

If 4 is unknown, defer public copy.

---

## 10. Section admission test

For every section:

```text
DONOR SECTION JOB
TARGET SECTION JOB
TARGET EVIDENCE CLASS
CLAIM CEILING
DISPOSITION
PROOF SOURCE
UNKNOWN FIELDS
```

A section contract is incomplete if it only specifies visual parity.

---

## 11. Interaction and 3D do not create facts

High-fidelity interaction and 3D replication are allowed under the selected replication mode.

But:

```text
INTERACTION ROLE
≠ BUSINESS CLAIM

3D OBJECT ROLE
≠ BUSINESS FACT
```

A Dayos-like 3D object may be recreated with VTKALL semantics only when those semantics come from the target Soul or evidence.

If exact semantic meaning is unresolved, preserve the donor's **role** in the contract and defer the target object definition.

---

## 12. Framework gate

The Target Truth Gate sits between target recovery/Soul and final target×donor mapping.

Canonical rule:

```text
RECOVER TARGET
↓
AUDIT TARGET
↓
EXTRACT SOUL
↓
FREEZE TARGET TRUTH BASELINE
↓
SELECT / APPLY REPLICATION MODE
↓
MAP DONOR STRUCTURE
↓
RUN SLOT-LEVEL TRUTH CHECK
↓
INK
```

The baseline is updated only with new target evidence or explicit target decisions.

---

## 13. VTKALL × Dayos learning

This case exposed the rule through Dayos routes such as:

```text
PLANS
PARTNERSHIP
```

VertikALL has a supported Method and a supported relationship philosophy.

It does **not** currently have verified target evidence for:

```text
Dayos-like pricing plans
formal partnership program
partner comparison model
partner result metrics
```

Therefore:

```text
DAYOS PLANS PAGE MODE
→ may inform VTKALL METHOD process geometry
→ must not create pricing plans

DAYOS PARTNERSHIP ROUTE
→ does not automatically create VTKALL PARTNERSHIP route
→ relationship philosophy may remain within supported target narrative
```

This is the first strong validation of the Target Truth Gate.
